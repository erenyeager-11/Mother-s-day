Five Wishes for You, Mama

I🌺

My First Home

Before I knew the world, I knew you. Your heartbeat was my lullaby, your arms my only universe. Thank you for being the warmth I always return to — my first home, my forever safe place.

II✨

Every Sacrifice Seen

Every sleepless night, every silent prayer, every thing you gave up without me ever asking — I see it all now. My heart is so full of gratitude it spills into everything I do.

III🌸

My Greatest Teacher

You taught me to be brave without being reckless, to love without holding back, to rise every time I fall. Everything good in me is a reflection of everything that is you.

IV💖

A Thousand Lifetimes

If I could be born a thousand times, in a thousand different worlds — I would spend every one of those lives finding my way back to you. Always, always you.

V🌹

Today & Always

May you feel today every ounce of joy that you have poured into me over all these years. You deserve the whole sky, the whole sea, and every flower in every garden.

A Letter for You

10th of May — Mother's Day

Dearest Mama,

There are feelings that live so deep inside me that no word, in any language, can truly reach them. But I try, because you deserve to know.

You are the reason I believe in goodness. You are proof that love is not just something we say — it is something we do, every single day, quietly and without applause.

I watch you and I see strength. I hear you and I feel peace. I think of you and I am home.

Today is yours. Every flower, every song, every golden moment of this day belongs to you. And tomorrow, and all the days after — you carry my whole heart with you always.

With all my love, forever

x o x o x o

I love you, Mama.

Happy Mother's Day — today, tomorrow & always 🌸
